A human body around 2 meters tall can actually interact with radio waves near the 2-meter amateur band.

The relationship comes from wavelength:

λ=c/f
	​


Where:

      λ = wavelength
      c = speed of light
      f = frequency
 The 2-Meter Amateur Band

The famous amateur radio VHF band:

around 144–148 MHz

has a wavelength close to:

λ≈2 meters

That is why it is called the 2-meter band.
